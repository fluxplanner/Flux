About us

A student-led organization

The Aorta Project is dedicated to bridging the gap between immigrant communities and the American healthcare system. We strive to deliver essential medical knowledge to the immigrant communities and youth who need it most, through our AED initiative: Advocate, Educate, Decode.

We saw families sitting in *waiting* *rooms,* nodding along to words they didn’t fully understand, too intimidated to ask questions in a system that never made space for them. So we created the *space* *ourselves.*

The AED initiative

Advocate. Educate. Decode.

A

Advocate

Fighting for immigrant families to be seen and heard in clinical spaces, and teaching families how to advocate for themselves with providers and ask questions.

E

Educate

Tutoring youth in heart health, with monthly workshops and lesson plans written by students: the curriculum we wish someone had handed those kids before they needed it.

D

Decode

Bilingual resource navigation, clinic connections, prescription assistance and insurance terms, plus a free online dictionary that translates confusing medical terms.

Decode

Medical dictionary

Confusing medical words, in plain language. Free, for immigrants, seniors and anyone who has left an appointment unsure what they were told.

    Our founders

    We aren’t special

    Just two motivated teens who kept watching the same thing happen in our own community.

    Arav Sheth

    Junior · Co-founder

    Hi, I’m Arav Sheth, a motivated junior and co-founder of The Aorta Project. I am very passionate about healthcare and serving my community in any way I can, and I am also very connected to my heritage. As part of an immigrant community myself, I kept seeing families around me struggle to navigate a healthcare system that was never made for them. So I decided to build something that was.

    Aryan Khedkar

    Junior · Co-founder

    Hi! I’m Aryan Khedkar, a junior and co-founder of The Aorta Project. As a member of a minority community myself, I have seen firsthand how difficult it is for minority and immigrant families to adapt and find the right support, especially if they have just immigrated to another country. Motivated to change that, we decided to build a system where this wouldn’t happen to them — and we’re just getting started.
