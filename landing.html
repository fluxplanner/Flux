For teachers, counselors & admins

A workspace for work.
A planner for life. One account.

Staff accounts flip between Work mode and Personal mode — lesson plans and caseload never mix with your grocery list, and your evenings stay yours.

Work mode School

Role-aware tools that follow your job title.

  • ✓Lesson Hub — today's classes bell by bell, lesson notes, attendance, material reminders and a sub-plan template.
  • ✓Classroom tools — quick-grade buckets, a fair student picker, a group maker, exit tickets and a big-screen timer.
  • ✓Messages — direct messages and group chats with colleagues.
  • ✓Counselor tools — what’s coming up, notes for every 1:1, parent call or IEP meeting, and crisis protocols.
  • ✓Resources — free, classroom-ready material from across the web, sorted by subject.

Personal mode Life

Everything after the last bell.

  • ✓Personal tasks — errands, appointments and life admin, fully separate from school.
  • ✓Personal hub — your own dashboard of what matters outside the building.
  • ✓Meeting notes & PD — decisions and action items, and your professional development hours.
  • ✓Wellbeing — check-ins and balance tracking, because burnout helps nobody.
  • ✓One tap to Google — Gmail, Drive, Docs, Calendar and Classroom from your workspace.
