For teachers, counselors & admins

A workspace for work.
A planner for life. One account.

Flux staff accounts flip between Work mode and Personal mode — your lesson plans and caseload never mix with your grocery list, and your evenings stay yours.

💼 Work mode

Role-aware tools that follow your job title.

  • ✓Lesson Hub — today's bell schedule, lesson notes and class-by-class planning.
  • ✓Gradebook & classes — rosters, grades and per-student progress at a glance.
  • ✓Counselor caseload — meetings from Google Calendar, student lists and caseload widgets.
  • ✓Admin operations — announcements, school calendar and user management.
  • ✓School feed & office hours — publish drop-in hours students can actually find.

🌿 Personal mode

Everything after the last bell.

  • ✓Personal tasks — errands, appointments and life admin, kept fully separate from school scope.
  • ✓Personal hub — your own dashboard of what matters outside the building.
  • ✓Wellbeing — check-ins and balance tracking, because burnout helps nobody.
  • ✓Professional development — track PD hours and growth goals on your own terms.
  • ✓Your Google, your way — personal Gmail, Drive and Calendar stay in personal mode.
