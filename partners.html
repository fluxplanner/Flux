How it works

A simple trade, written down so both sides know what they get.

1

We build it

A real app made for your mission, built and hosted on Flux at no cost. No ads, no account needed, and your users’ data stays theirs.

2

It stays yours

Your name, your colours, your logo. Flux lists it on the hub with a Partner label, and a small “Powered by Flux” credit is the only Flux branding inside.

3

You spread the word

Share Flux with your community: a post, a newsletter mention, a link on your site. The kit below has the words, logos and posts ready to go.

One line

Flux is the free planner for school — your classes, Canvas assignments, calendar and study tools in one calm place.

Short

Flux is a free planner built for real school life. It puts your classes, Canvas assignments and calendar in one place, breaks big projects into a little each day, and comes with 118 interactive study tools across 16 subjects. There are free tools anyone can open with no account: a grapher, a periodic table, flashcards, a TI-84-style calculator and more.

About the partnership

Synara is built and hosted by Flux, the free planner for school. Flux made Synara for us as part of its partner program, so students with epilepsy can track their medication and seizures and carry a safety card anyone can follow. If you’re a student, Flux is worth a look for your classes and study tools: https://fluxplanner.github.io/Flux/

A few ground rules

  • Call it Flux or Flux Planner, and link to fluxplanner.github.io/Flux.
  • Use the logo as it is: don’t recolour, stretch or crop it.
  • Flux builds and hosts partner apps; what a partner app says — like medical information — is the partner’s own.
  • Questions about the kit? Email Flux.
