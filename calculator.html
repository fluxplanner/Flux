Calculator

TI-84-style graphing calculator

Choose the Plus CE or Evo-style layout. Flux84 includes MathPrint, graphing, tables, statistics, finance, matrices and programs. It does not yet support every TI app or hardware feature.

On a keyboard

  • Click the calculator, then type numbers and + - * / ^ ( )
  • Enter calculates, ⌫ deletes, Esc clears
  • Letters type as alpha: A–Z
  • Arrow keys move, and ↑ on the home screen picks an earlier answer

Good to know

  • 2nd then a key gives the blue function above it; alpha gives the green letter.
  • Everything you store stays in this browser, like the calculator's memory. 2nd mem resets it.
  • Short answers stay in ordinary decimal form, even in Sci or Eng mode. Exponents appear when the decimal would be long.
