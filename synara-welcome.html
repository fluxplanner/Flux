What it does

Three things, done properly

Built for the moments that matter: the dose you nearly forgot, the appointment where your doctor asks “how has it been?”, and the minute a teacher needs to know what to do.

Medication on time

Dose reminders, one-tap logging, and a history of taken, late and missed doses you can print for your doctor.

A seizure log that notices

Log a seizure in seconds. Synara looks for patterns — missed doses, sleep, stress — and says plainly what is a pattern and what isn’t.

A safety card anyone can follow

Step-by-step first aid with a seizure timer, your rescue plan and who to call — one tap away, even offline.

Private by design

Your health record is yours

Synara works entirely on your device. Sign in with Flux and it syncs between your devices — encrypted before it leaves, so not even Flux can read it.

  • End-to-end encrypted sync. You keep a private sync key and enter it once on each new device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no selling data. Ever.
  • Calm by design: nothing flashes or flickers, for students who are photosensitive.
  • Optional: show dose times in your Flux Planner calendar.
