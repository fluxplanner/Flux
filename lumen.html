About

What is Project Lumen?

Project Lumen is a student led initiative aiming to spread cancer awareness, and bring light to the untold stories of the many people battling cancer today.

My mission

Project Lumen was created with one mission: bring *awareness,* *education* and spark meaningful *conversations* about cancer, bringing the *light* needed to make a *difference.*

Awareness into action

Three ways we bring light

Awareness

Making sure cancer is something people see and talk about, all year and not only on awareness days.

Education

Clear, honest information about cancer, its signs and how to support the people living with it. Details soon

Conversation

Every voice heard and every story told: space for patients, survivors and families to share what they have been through.
